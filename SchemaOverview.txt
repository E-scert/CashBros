
1. Schema Overview
   - Purpose of the database (stokvel savings system).
   - List of tables: `users`, `user_account`, `user_transact`, `goal`.

2. **Table Definitions**
   - Each table with columns, data types, constraints, and relationships.
   - Example:  
     - `users`: PK = `user_id`, stores member info.  
     - `user_account`: 1:1 with users, unique `account_num`.  
     - `user_transact`: 1:N with accounts, linked to goals.  
     - `goal`: savings target, auto‑updated totals.

3. Constraints & Rules
   - One user → one account (unique FK).  
   - Withdrawals blocked until goal matured (trigger).  
   - Deposits auto‑update totals (trigger).  

4. Business Logic Notes
   - Only deposits allowed until goal is matured.  
   - Goals flip status to `matured` once target reached.  
   - Cascade deletes ensure data integrity.
